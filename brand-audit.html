Hypothesis

The millions of AI-curious people aged 22 to 35 watching AI in Context could convert into newsletter subscribers far more cheaply through a free, actionable lead magnet than through a cold book purchase ask. The audience is already there. The missing piece is a bridge from "that was interesting" to "here's my email."

What I'd Do

One fix for today. One new campaign.

Fix Existing

Add a third creative territory with a free conversion path

Your book ads run two angles that say similar things. Test a third: lead with AI anxiety ("Which of your skills survive the next ten years?") and convert on a free first chapter or a 2 minute interactive career check instead of a $20 book. You'll capture the curious people your current funnel loses.

New Campaign

Launch Google Search ads on high-intent queries

300+ GDN creatives, zero Search ads. Bid on "will AI take my job," "best career advice book," and branded terms. Drive to the free chapter or the check, gate on an email. These people are looking for what you offer. Show up where they're searching.

The experiment

Run both for six weeks at ~$40K total. Measure cost per qualified subscriber (someone still opening emails at 90 days), not raw signups. If that number comes in under $5, scale the winner. If nothing clears the bar, the learning about which message doesn't work is the valuable output.
